Chocolate Cake Recipe

A simple and delicious homemade chocolate cake.

Recipe Information

Preparation Time: 20 minutes

Cooking Time: 35 minutes

Servings: 8 people

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of flour
  • 1 cup of sugar
  • 1/2 cup of cocoa powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup of milk
  • 1/2 cup of butter
  • 1 teaspoon of baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon of vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix the flour, cocoa powder, sugar, and baking powder.
  3. Add the eggs, milk, butter, and vanilla extract.
  4. Mix everything until the batter is smooth.
  5. Pour the batter into a baking pan.
  6. Bake for about 35 minutes.
  7. Allow the cake to cool before serving.
